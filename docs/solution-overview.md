# Solution Overview: SIH26091

## Proposed Platform Concept
The proposed platform is an **AI-powered hyper-local business advisory and financial structuring platform** engineered specifically for rural micro-entrepreneurs. It integrates spatial data analytics, financial modeling engines, government scheme databases, and an AI agent to turn complex market data into clear, actionable business recommendations.

## 🔑 Key Features & Modules

### 1. Hyper-Local Competitor Discovery Engine
* Analyzes commercial density around a specified pin code, village, or town radius.
* Merges official MSME registered business data with geospatial points of interest (POI) from OpenStreetMap.
* Incorporates ground-truth reports submitted by local community members to account for informal businesses.

### 2. Automated Financial Feasibility & Credit Model
* Accepts user's planned investment budget and proposed business category.
* Calculates estimated fixed capital, initial inventory/working capital, monthly operational costs, expected revenue curves, and break-even timelines.
* Generates loan repayment schedules (EMI) and sensitivity scenarios (pessimistic vs. realistic vs. optimistic).

### 3. Government Scheme Matching & Subsidy Engine
* Matches applicant profile (age, gender, social category, location type, project cost, sector) against active subsidy schemes.
* Highlights margin money requirements, loan coverage, and nodal agency submission guidelines.

### 4. Deterministic AI Business Advisor
* Functions as an intelligent interface that calls backend calculation tools and GIS APIs rather than hallucinating text.
* Delivers simple, regional language summaries explaining whether a business idea is viable, overcrowded, or financially risky.

---

## 🔄 User Workflow

1. **Input Parameters:** Entrepreneur enters proposed business type, target location (Pin Code / Village), available personal capital, and demographic details.
2. **Data Aggregation:** System pulls nearby competitors, census/demographic estimates, and relevant government scheme guidelines.
3. **Analytics Execution:**
   - Spatial engine calculates competition score.
   - Financial engine computes capital gap and EMI options.
   - Scheme engine identifies applicable subsidies.
4. **Advisory Report & AI Guidance:** Platform generates a plain-language viability summary, detailed financial statement, and step-by-step scheme application guide.
