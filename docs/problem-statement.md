# Problem Statement: SIH26091

## Problem Title
**AI-Powered Hyper-Local Business Advisory and Financial Structuring Platform for Rural Micro-Entrepreneurs**

---

## 🔍 Context & Background

Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) represent the backbone of India's rural economy, driving local employment, poverty reduction, and decentralized economic growth. However, aspiring micro-entrepreneurs in tier-3+, rural, and semi-urban regions face steep barriers when starting or scaling a enterprise.

While urban founders leverage sophisticated market research tools, business consultants, and formal credit institutions, rural entrepreneurs rely heavily on informal networks, personal intuition, or unstructured advice. This leads to high rates of business failure, misallocated capital, and underutilization of public support systems.

---

## 🚨 Core Challenges Faced by Rural Micro-Entrepreneurs

### 1. Hyper-Local Market Opacity & Competition Risks
* Rural entrepreneurs often replicate nearby successful businesses (e.g., opening another grocery store or Kirana on a street already saturated with Kirana shops).
* Lack of accessible spatial and demographic data means entrepreneurs cannot quantify local demand, customer density, or unserved service gaps.

### 2. Financial Structuring & Debt Risk
* Inability to build structured business plans or calculate critical financial indicators (e.g., Working Capital Needs, Break-Even Point, Net Profit Margin, Debt-Service Coverage Ratio).
* High vulnerability to predatory informal lending due to failure in completing formal bank credit appraisals.

### 3. Scheme Awareness & Application Friction
* Central and State governments offer numerous financial aid and subsidy initiatives (such as PMEGP, PM-Mudra Yojana, Stand-Up India, PM-FME).
* Information regarding eligibility, subsidy percentages, mandatory documentation, and nodal bank processes remains fragmented and complex for first-time applicants.

---

## 🎯 Target Beneficiaries

* **Rural Micro-Entrepreneurs:** Artisans, small retailers, agri-processing units, local repair services, and cottage industry founders.
* **Self-Help Groups (SHGs) & Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs):** Groups planning collective micro-enterprises.
* **Grassroots Advisory Centers:** District Industries Centres (DICs), Common Service Centre (CSC) operators, and local bank facilitators assisting rural applicants.

---

## 🏁 Desired Outcomes & Impact

1. **Reduced Business Failure Rates:** By providing data-backed market density maps and feasibility alerts before capital deployment.
2. **Improved Creditworthiness:** By generating standardized, bank-ready financial reports and EMI projections.
3. **Maximizing Government Subsidy Take-up:** By automatically matching applicants with relevant government programs to lower initial capital costs.
